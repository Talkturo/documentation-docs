## Overview

To get started, you'll need a Talkturo account and a project.

## Steps

1. Sign up at `https://dashboard.talkturo.com`
2. Click "Create Project"
3. Choose a voice agent template or build custom

## Deploy the Agent

1. Enter a phone number for outbound calls
2. Click "Deploy"
3. Monitor calls in the dashboard

<Callout kind="tip">Check analytics for live insight.</Callout>
