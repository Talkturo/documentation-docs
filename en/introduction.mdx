## Welcome to Talkturo

Talkturo is a voice AI platform for deploying AI-powered phone agents that handle real inbound and outbound conversations. You can configure assistants with custom prompts, voices, and language models, run calling campaigns with scheduling and retry logic, and manage customer data, call activity, and automation from one workspace.

Teams use Talkturo to combine telephony, CRM, knowledge retrieval, workflow automation, and analytics into a single operating layer for voice. That includes call logs with transcripts and recordings, AI-powered intelligence, a built-in CRM for contacts, companies, opportunities, and custom fields, and tools for managing telephony through Telnyx and Twilio.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Talkturo also includes TurboFlow for visual workflow automation, a knowledge base for retrieval-based answers during calls, a voice library with custom voice cloning, and Close AI, a desktop sales assistant app for calling workflows.
</Callout>
