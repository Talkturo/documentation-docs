## Understand your billing model

Talkturo bills usage with **credits**, not direct per-call invoices. Your workspace balance decreases as calls run, and you can add credits separately from your subscription plan.

Subscription plans and credits serve different purposes. A plan controls product access and included monthly credits, while top-ups add spendable credits for voice usage when you need more capacity.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Credits are consumed in real time as calls happen. If your workflows depend on phone calls, keep enough credits available or enable auto-reload to avoid interruptions.
</Callout>

## How credits work

Each workspace has a credit balance that supports fractional usage. Talkturo can deduct small amounts such as `0.025` credits for short calls or partial usage, so your balance reflects actual consumption instead of rounding every event to whole numbers.

New workspaces start with **10 free credits**. Talkturo adds and removes credits through a small set of transaction types, which appear in your billing history.

### Credit exchange rate

Talkturo uses a base exchange rate of **$1 USD = 2 credits**. That exchange rate also applies to standard top-ups and auto-reload calculations unless a bonus credit promotion applies.

### Transaction types

| Transaction type | What it means                                            |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| `purchase`       | Credits added after a paid top-up                        |
| `usage`          | Credits consumed by calls and related usage              |
| `bonus`          | Promotional or extra credits added to the workspace      |
| `refund`         | Credits returned after an adjustment                     |
| `signup_bonus`   | Initial free credits granted when a workspace is created |
| `reset`          | Administrative credit balance reset or correction        |

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Because credits support fractional amounts, small calls and partial minutes do not overcharge your workspace. You pay for the underlying usage pattern, not a rounded bundle.
</Callout>

## Add credit top-ups

Top-ups increase your available usage balance beyond the credits included in your subscription. This is the amount you draw down during voice activity.

| Package       | Price       | Credits added                                               |
| ------------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Starter Pack  | $99         | 198                                                         |
| Pro Pack      | $349        | 698                                                         |
| Business Pack | $499        | 998                                                         |
| Custom top-up | Minimum $99 | Any amount above the minimum, converted at the current rate |

## See how calls consume credits

Call cost depends on the components your assistant uses. Standard voice pipelines may charge each service separately, while realtime mode uses a single bundled per-minute rate.

### Standard usage components

| Component             | Billing unit         | Notes                                        |
| --------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Speech-to-Text        | Per minute           | Charged for transcribing live call audio     |
| LLM prompt tokens     | Per 1,000 tokens     | Charged for model input                      |
| LLM completion tokens | Per 1,000 tokens     | Charged for model output                     |
| Text-to-Speech        | Per 1,000 characters | Charged for generated spoken output          |
| Telephony             | Per minute           | Charged for phone connectivity and call time |

### Realtime mode

Realtime mode uses a **single per-minute rate** instead of separate Speech-to-Text, LLM, and Text-to-Speech line items. That makes billing simpler when your assistant runs on a realtime stack.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  A call with heavier model output or longer assistant responses consumes more LLM tokens and TTS characters, so two calls with the same duration may cost different amounts.
</Callout>

## Configure auto-reload

Auto-reload keeps your workspace funded by charging your saved payment method when the credit balance falls below a threshold. This is the safest option if you run production phone numbers or scheduled outbound activity.

### Auto-reload settings

<ParamField body="enabled" param-type="boolean" required="true" deprecated="false" show-location="false">
  Turn auto-reload on or off for the workspace.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="amount" param-type="number" required="true" deprecated="false" show-location="false">
  Dollar amount to charge on each reload. The default is `$10`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="threshold" param-type="number" required="true" deprecated="false" show-location="false">
  Credit balance that triggers a reload. The default threshold is `10` credits.
</ParamField>

### How reload credits are calculated

Talkturo converts a reload charge into credits with:

`max(1, round(amount_usd × 2))`

For example, a **$10** auto-reload adds **20 credits**.

### Auto-reload requirements and behavior

- Auto-reload requires a saved **default payment method**
- Charges run through **Stripe invoicing**
- A successful reload can reactivate **suspended phone numbers**
- Reload happens automatically when the balance drops below the configured threshold

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  If no default payment method is available, Talkturo cannot complete an auto-reload. Low balances may interrupt phone-based workflows until you add a payment method or top up manually.
</Callout>

## Understand desktop app plans

Close AI desktop access is billed separately from workspace voice credits. These plans control desktop seats, not call-credit balances for voice agents.

| Desktop plan       | Monthly price            | Seats  | Notes                            |
| ------------------ | ------------------------ | ------ | -------------------------------- |
| Desktop Starter    | $186/month               | 1      | Single-seat plan                 |
| Desktop Pro        | $373/month               | 3      | Expandable with additional seats |
| Desktop Enterprise | Custom                   | Custom | Negotiable pricing and terms     |
| Additional seat    | $99/month or $1,188/year | 1 each | Add-on seat pricing              |

If your team uses both desktop coaching and voice agents, manage these as separate budget lines: desktop seats for users, credits for call execution.

## Manage payment methods and invoices

Stripe is the primary billing provider for Talkturo. Saved cards are used for purchases and auto-reload, and invoices are available as downloadable PDFs.

### What you can expect

- **Saved cards** support automatic reloads
- **Stripe invoices** provide a billing record for purchases
- **PDF invoices** are available for download

## Claim credit grants

Talkturo can attach credits to purchases made through **agent payment links**. These credits are issued as grants and move through a simple lifecycle before they affect a workspace balance.

### Grant lifecycle

| Status      | Meaning                                                    |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| `unclaimed` | The credit grant exists but is not assigned to a workspace |
| `claimed`   | The grant has been applied to a workspace                  |

### How grants are used

Workspace owners or admins can claim a grant into a workspace from the dashboard. When the payment link supports buy-and-clone behavior, Talkturo can also clone the source assistant into the destination workspace during the claim flow.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  If you sell assistants through payment links, grants can fund usage immediately after purchase and reduce setup work by cloning the original assistant into the buyer's workspace.
</Callout>

## Keep billing predictable

A few habits make credit usage easier to manage:

- Enable **auto-reload** before launching production calling
- Review **per-call cost breakdowns** to understand which components drive spend
- Separate **plan budgeting** from **credit budgeting**
- Watch for **bonus credits** when distributing agent payment links
- Keep a valid **default payment method** on file
