## Close AI helps your team coach live calls and review performance after the call ends

Close AI is Talkturo's desktop sales assistant for macOS and Windows. It gives your team a dedicated workspace for real-time call coaching, post-call session analysis, script guidance, and prompt management without working inside the web dashboard during a conversation.

The desktop app connects to your Talkturo workspace after sign-in, so sessions, scripts, prompts, and analytics stay tied to the same team account. Sales reps use it during live calls, and managers use the connected dashboard views to review coaching trends across the team.

<Callout kind="info">

Download the desktop app from the Close AI area in your dashboard at `/home/[account]/close-ai`. After you sign in with your Talkturo credentials, the app authenticates through OAuth and connects to your workspace automatically.

</Callout>

## Download and installation

Close AI is available for both macOS and Windows. The app is distributed from the Close AI section of your Talkturo account so each installation stays connected to the correct workspace and plan.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Close AI in your dashboard" icon="monitor">

  Go to the Close AI area in Talkturo at `/home/[account]/close-ai`. The page includes desktop download actions for macOS and Windows.

  A successful visit shows the Close AI navigation with tabs for Sessions, Dashboard, Script, Settings, and Prompts.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the desktop app" icon="download">

  Choose the installer for your operating system. Download the macOS app if your team uses Apple devices, or the Windows app if your team works on Windows laptops or desktops.

  After the download finishes, you should have a local installer file ready to open.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Talkturo" icon="lock">

  Open the installer, complete the local install flow, and sign in with your Talkturo credentials. Close AI uses OAuth for authentication, so your desktop session stays tied to your Talkturo account.

  When sign-in succeeds, the app connects to your workspace and loads the desktop experience for that account.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">

If your team manages multiple workspaces, confirm you are signed into the correct Talkturo account before starting a call. Sessions, prompts, and analytics are stored at the workspace level.

</Callout>

## Sessions support two coaching modes

Close AI sessions are the center of the desktop workflow. Every session is created in one of two modes, and the mode changes how the rep interacts with the assistant during the call.

### Listen mode

Listen mode uses the microphone path to follow live call audio and provide real-time coaching. It is designed for active sales conversations where the rep wants guidance without stopping to type or ask for help manually.

In the session list, Listen mode appears with a mic icon. Teams typically use it for live objection handling, pacing support, and checking whether the rep is following the planned script.

### Ask mode

Ask mode works as a co-pilot Q and A experience. The rep asks the assistant questions during a live conversation and gets direct help without switching into full coaching mode.

In the session list, Ask mode appears with a message icon. Teams often use it when reps need quick answers, phrasing help, or context-sensitive prompts during a call.

## CloseScore gives each session a clear performance signal

CloseScore is Talkturo's session score from 0 to 100. It appears in session analysis and across dashboard analytics so reps and managers can compare individual calls and broader team trends using the same scoring model.

The score is color-coded to make results scannable:

| Score range | Color |
| --- | --- |
| 80 and above | Emerald |
| 60 to 79 | Yellow |
| 40 to 59 | Orange |
| Below 40 | Red |

CloseScore is not a single summary number with no detail behind it. Each analyzed session also includes a category breakdown with sub-items and notes, which makes it easier to see why a score changed and what the rep should improve next.

## Session analysis shows how the call unfolded

Session analysis turns a completed conversation into practical coaching feedback. It combines speaking metrics, qualitative feedback, script adherence, and prospect signals in one place so reps can review a call quickly and managers can coach from evidence instead of memory.

### Talk metrics

These four metrics describe how the rep handled the flow of the conversation.

| Metric | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| Talk Share | The percentage of total call time the salesperson spoke |
| WPM | Words per minute, used as a pacing signal |
| Patience | The percentage of pauses that were well timed |
| Longest Monologue | The longest uninterrupted speech segment |

A balanced set of talk metrics helps teams spot common issues fast. For example, high talk share combined with long monologues can point to weak discovery, while low patience can point to interrupting or rushing the prospect.

### Coaching feedback

Every analyzed session includes coaching cards for **Strengths** and **Improvements**. Strengths identify what the rep did well, while Improvements combine weaknesses and risks into a single area the rep can act on next.

Because the feedback sits next to transcripts and score details, reps can move from summary to evidence without opening a separate tool. That makes post-call review faster and easier to turn into repeatable coaching.

### Script coverage

If your team uses a Close AI script, session analysis shows how much of the script the rep covered during the call. Each section is checked against the conversation so you can see which parts were addressed, skipped, or only partially covered.

The checklist also shows time spent in each section. That helps managers answer two common coaching questions: whether the rep followed the intended flow, and whether too much time was spent on the wrong stage of the conversation.

### Prospect personality analysis

Close AI also identifies a prospect personality type and summarizes how to work with that style. The analysis includes the personality label, the levers that tend to motivate that prospect, and the behaviors or phrases that may create friction.

This view is useful when a rep wants to adjust tone for follow-up calls, or when managers want to explain why a conversation stalled even though the script was technically covered.

## The desktop dashboard tracks team performance across eight views

The desktop dashboard rolls session data up to the team level. It is designed for managers, enablement leads, and reps who want to compare performance over time instead of reviewing one call at a time.

### KPI cards and time ranges

The dashboard starts with four KPI cards:

| KPI | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Calls in period | Total calls for the selected time range |
| Avg CloseScore | Average session score across the selected range |
| Avg Duration | Average session length |
| Active Team Members | Number of team members with activity in the range |

Use the time range selector to switch between `7d`, `30d`, and `90d`. This makes it easier to compare short-term coaching progress against longer performance trends.

### Analysis tabs

Each tab answers a different coaching or management question.

| Tab | What you can review |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Weekly call and CloseScore trends, duration distribution, and leaderboard data |
| CloseScore | Score trends, distribution buckets, and per-member scoring |
| Objections | Objection counts, categories, types, intensity, and score correlation |
| Questions | Open, deep, and closed question mix plus missed questions |
| Personality | Prospect personality distribution and per-member patterns |
| Language | Talk share, WPM, patience, and longest monologue trends |
| Coaching | Evaluations, top strengths, weaknesses, and risks |
| Script | Script usage, average coverage, order deviation, and block-level coverage |

### AI narrative insights

The dashboard can generate written insights and recommendations based on the active analysis tab. This feature turns chart data into a plain-language summary, which is useful when a manager needs to brief a team quickly or explain what changed across a time period.

Use the narrative output as a coaching shortcut, then open the underlying tab data when you need more detail. That sequence keeps reviews fast while preserving the evidence behind the recommendation.

## Settings and prompts control how the assistant behaves

Close AI separates workspace-level AI behavior from personal prompt presets. That split helps teams keep core coaching behavior consistent while still giving individual reps room to tailor how they work.

### Settings for system prompts

The Settings area lets your team customize seven desktop AI system prompts. These prompts control how Close AI behaves in areas such as live coaching, post-call analysis, Ask mode, sales suggestions, summaries, assistant responses, and interview support.

Workspace settings follow a clear fallback order: workspace override, admin standard, then Talkturo built-in default. That structure lets admins standardize coaching while still allowing changes when the team needs a different voice or workflow.

### Personal prompt presets

The Prompts area is a personal library for reusable prompt presets in Ask mode. Reps can save frequently used instructions, mark one prompt as the default, and update or remove prompts as their workflow changes.

This setup is especially useful for teams that want consistency without forcing every rep into the same phrasing. Shared coaching rules can live in Settings, while individual working styles can live in Prompts.
