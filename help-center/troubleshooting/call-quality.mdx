---
title: Voice Agent Call Quality Issues
description: How to fix low audio quality or dropped calls.
---

## Symptoms

- Calls sound distorted
- Voice agent is hard to hear
- Calls drop unexpectedly

## Possible Causes

- Network instability
- Low bandwidth
- Outdated browser

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your network">
    ````bash
    ping api.talkturo.com
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch network">
    Try a wired or stable WiFi connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update browser">
    Use the latest version of Chrome or Firefox.
  </Step>
</Steps>
